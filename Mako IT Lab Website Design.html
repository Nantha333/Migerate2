THE MIGRATION CHALLENGE

Moving data is easy.
Moving it correctly is not.

Enterprise data rarely exists as isolated records.

Customer information connects to transactions. Products connect to pricing. Employees connect to payroll and history. Applications depend on references that may not be visible at first glance.

When those relationships are missed, problems appear long after the data has already moved.

01

Relationships

Preserve the connections between records, systems, and business entities so nothing arrives orphaned.

02

Transformations

Map and transform data across different structures, formats, and business rules.

03

Historical Data

Carry forward the history the business still needs for reporting, compliance, and decisions.

04

References & Dependencies

Identify the dependencies connecting applications, workflows, documents, and data.

05

Attachments

Migrate supporting files and linked assets without losing their associations.

06

Data Quality

Identify duplicates, missing values, and invalid records before they become migration problems.

THE BUSINESS RISK

A migration can fail long after the data moves.

A successful migration isn’t measured by how many records reached the target system.

It’s measured by whether the business can keep operating with confidence after cutover.

01

Broken relationships

Records arrive, but the connections between customers, transactions, products, or processes are no longer reliable.

02

Lost history

Historical information is incomplete, inaccessible, or disconnected from current records.

03

Unreconciled data

Source and target systems contain different totals, records, or values — with no clear explanation.

04

Business disruption

Migration issues affect users, applications, reporting, or critical operations.

05

Untrusted data

The system is technically live, but teams can’t confidently trust the information inside it.

The goal isn’t simply to complete the migration.
It’s to make the new environment trustworthy from day one.

OUR MIGRATION APPROACH

From discovery to validated data.

We treat migration as an engineering discipline, not a one-time data transfer.

Every migration is planned around the structure of the data, the dependencies around it, and the continuity the business expects at cutover.

01

Discover

Map source systems, structures, relationships, dependencies, volumes, and quality issues before anything moves.

02

Map

Build source-to-target mappings, identify transformation rules, and define the target-state structure.

03

Transform

Clean, normalize, deduplicate, and restructure data to meet target-system and business rules.

04

Validate

Check records, relationships, references, attachments, and rules against agreed acceptance criteria.

05

Reconcile

Reconcile source and target, resolve exceptions, and investigate differences.

06

Cut Over

Execute a controlled production migration within an agreed window.
